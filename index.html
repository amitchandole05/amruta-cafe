Our Story

Brewed with Heart since 2018

Amruta Cafe was born from a dream — to create a space where every cup tells a story and every bite feels like home. Founded in Pune, we've grown from a tiny corner shop to a beloved community hub.

We source our beans directly from small farms in Coorg and Chikmagalur, ensuring every sip is as ethical as it is delicious.
